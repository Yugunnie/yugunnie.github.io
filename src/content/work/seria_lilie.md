## Intense In All Aspects

The Lilies Series consists of 4 flower heads, 2 flower buds, 5 leaves and 1 part of the stem. The inspiration comes from the eroded cliffs and stone formations of the coast.

Each piece is unique, with textures created by pressing local stones and shells into the soft clay before firing.
