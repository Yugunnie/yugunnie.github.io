## The Meaning of Color

When creating this series, I mostly looked at white roses as my personal favorites. However throught block printing each indvidal rose we can get a multitude of colors. To each their own, roses fo different colors carry a variaty of meanings. What color would you choose?

> “She cast her fragrance and her radiance over me. I ought never to have run away from her... I ought to have guessed all the affection that lay behind her poor little stratagems. Flowers are so inconsistent! But I was too young to know how to love her...”
― Antoine de Saint-Exupéry, The Little Prince

The more colors the more time is needed for printing.
